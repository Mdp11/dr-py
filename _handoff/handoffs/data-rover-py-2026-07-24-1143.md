## Standing Constraints
- OR shape is a **flat `any_of` group** (matches if ANY member matches; top level stays AND) — user chose it over a full boolean tree (UI/evaluator weight for unproven need) and over multi-value criteria (can't OR across properties).
- Members are **any leaf criterion, no nesting** — enforced structurally by typing members as the leaf-only union (`LeafCriterion`); no custom validator.
- **Empty group = no-op (matches everything)** on BOTH sides — deliberate override of `any([]) is False`, so mid-edit saves never blank a table/navigation (mirrors the unconfigured `NavigationSource` stance).
- **No schema_version bumps** (navigation stays 3, table stays 1) — additive change, self-contained app.
- Navigation existence-gating recurses per member inside `_match_nav_criterion` itself, NOT via the shared matcher — `core/search` semantics must stay byte-identical for `/model/search`.
- Vacuous-true inapplicable criteria inside a group are handled at the EDITING layer (recursive `pruneCriteria`, drop emptied groups), not in the matchers — keeps JS/Python parity untouched.
- UX: "+ OR group" button in `ScopeEditor`/`FilterStepRow`; the dialog's existing add-criterion dropdown gains an "Any of" item (no separate button). No convert-row↔group affordance (user declined).
- JS-parity contract: every semantic change lands in both `core/search/criteria.py` and `frontend/src/lib/search/evaluate.ts` with mirrored tests.
- User chose **subagent-driven execution** (fresh subagent per task, review between tasks).
