## Standing Constraints

  the O(model) cost somewhere else — a per-mutation reindex that makes every commit pay is a
  worse trade than the status quo. Measure the sweep's per-chunk time before/after.
- **K-21 (new):** the snapshot reader branches on the gzip magic, **never** on the key — never
  add a key-suffix branch, an `encoding` column, a migration or a backfill. `serialize
  .iter_model_json` (indented) stays the `/model/save` + `/model/download` save-file contract;
  `iter_model_json_compact` is the snapshot sibling. The rebind-forced, evict and baseline

  rebind sites.

## Deferred — Do Not Do
- **zstd for snapshots** (`compression.zstd` IS in this Python 3.14 build, 1.5.7) — considered
  during K-21 planning and declined by the planning session: gzip is the spec's proposal and is
  universal, the encoder is the larger cost, and the saving is ~0.4 s per write at scale 320.
  Recorded in the spec's K-21 non-goals.
- **Serialize outside the mutex by shallow-copying every properties dict under it** — declined
  for the +100–200 MB transient at 320k on a prediction. It is the upgrade path *if* the
  snapshot job's mutex hold ever proves to contend in practice.
- **An `encoding` column on `Snapshot` / a migration rewriting blobs** — declined; bytes
  sniffing covers it.
- **A `snapshot_gzip_level` setting** — declined; it is a module constant (`SNAPSHOT_GZIP_LEVEL
  = 3`), nobody needs to tune it per deployment.
- **A "skip the evict snapshot when a row at `model_rev` already exists" shortcut** — declined
  as YAGNI.
- Findings the K-21 final review explicitly accepted as-is — do not "fix" them: `decode_snapshot`
  returning `Any`; the `>= 3 chunks` assertion in `test_snapshot_codec.py`; the tautological
  `assert s.snapshot_sync is True` in `test_settings.py`; the `if sync if sync is not None else
  …` expression (copied verbatim from `search_index_build.start_search_index_build`, deliberately);
  the delete-project FK race (now a logged warning, was an unguarded 500).
- Everything K-20/K-6 already deferred: posting-set shrinking, `entity_states` backfill,
