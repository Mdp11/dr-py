- **SHA-256 in pure TypeScript**, not WebCrypto: the fold must be synchronous. CRC32 was rejected because it is linear, so an XOR fold cannot see two ids swapping `rev`s. `h(id, rev)` = first 8 bytes of SHA-256(`utf8(id) ‖ 0x00 ‖ ascii(decimal rev)`); the digest is the XOR over all entities, 16 lower-case hex digits (spec §7). The engine's hash must match `tests/api/test_state_digest.py`'s vectors and `engine/test/golden/digest.ts`, and becomes `WorkingCopy`'s default `entityHash`.
- **Server writers keep emitting snapshot v1 until sub-project B.** Plan 4 only reads v2 in the engine.
