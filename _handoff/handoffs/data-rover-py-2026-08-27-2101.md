## Deferred — Do Not Do
- **Paging `GET /model/relationships`** (adding `limit`/`offset` + a `RelationshipPage`
  return) — decided against in the design, not merely deferred: a third relationship-listing
  surface with no caller, whose `type` filter would still scan and whose other two filters
  duplicate `GET /model/elements/{id}/relationships` with a worse contract.
- **A `relationships_by_type` index on `IndexSet`** — declined; a fifth per-type structure
  for one scanning caller at 3.9 ms.
- **Deleting `POST`/`DELETE /model/relationships` or their client wrappers** — out of scope;
  they are the documented legacy mutation pair and three test modules call them. That the
  client wrappers are likewise test-only is a separate, non-performance cleanup.
- **A global response-side cap on unbounded reads** — declined; `GET /model` and
  `PUT /model/snapshot` already carry `deprecated=True` and `GET /model/download` streams.
- **Touching `core/search/criteria.py`** — `/model/search` must stay byte-identical.
- Everything K-24/K-23/K-22/K-21/K-20/K-6 deferred: returning insertion order from
  `_scope_ids`, a maintained sorted-id index, a per-rev cache of the untyped id list, a
  criterion-shaped fast path in the shared matcher, a bulk
  `restore_element(..., properties=...)`, diffing refs/rekey/roots per property, the `zip`
  trigram idiom, per-field trigram storage, bypassing hooks under `search_ready=False`,
  folding the sequence number into `uniq_groups`, a sequence field on `Element`, the
  per-sweep position-map hoist, zstd, an `encoding` column, serialize-outside-the-mutex,
  posting-set shrinking, `entity_states` backfill, journal-based `GET /commits/{rev}/model`.
