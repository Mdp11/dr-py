## Known Issues, Not Yet Fixed
- **`ENTITY_STATES_MAX` is an entity-count cap, not a byte cap** (`api/commit_states.py:41`).
  An update-heavy 5000-entity batch stores 10 000 full entity states while the `ops` column
  stores one-key patches. At the observed ~260 B/element that is ~2.6 MB — safe, and no worse
  in kind than the existing `inverse_ops` column for a 5000-victim delete — but a model with
  large property bags could push one row to tens of MB. Flagged by the final K-6 review as a
  forward-looking note, deliberately not fixed. Natural home is K-21/K-22 territory.
